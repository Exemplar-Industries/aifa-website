Brandon Patino is the founder of AI Film Academy and Exemplar Industries. He discovered AI filmmaking tools in 2023 and within months was producing cinematic short films entirely with AI — no camera, no crew, no studio.

He went on to set a Guinness World Record in AI-generated media and has been building a community of AI filmmakers ever since.

  • Built a community of hundreds of AI filmmakers
  • Posts daily AI filmmaking job opportunities from his professional network
