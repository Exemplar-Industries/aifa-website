The Manifesto

Principle 2: Finish Things

02
Principle
"A finished 7/10 film is infinitely more valuable than an unfinished 10/10 masterpiece."
  • Perfectionism is the Enemy The tools will always get better. If you wait for the "perfect" model, you will never publish anything.
  • The Final 10% is Where You Learn The most important learning happens in the final stages of a project: editing, sound design, and exporting.
  • Build a Habit of Shipping Publish your work, gather feedback, and apply those lessons to the next project. Volume leads to quality.
