The #1 Mistake

Too Many Tools,
Not Enough Workflow

There are hundreds of AI tools competing for your attention. New ones launch every week. Most creators get stuck in "tool paralysis" — constantly experimenting but never finishing anything.

The solution is not more tools. It is a repeatable, professional workflow that works regardless of which tools change.

  • Tool updates are constant — your workflow should be evergreen.
  • The 3-step workflow (Pre-Production → Production → Post-Production) never changes.
  • We focus on Google Flow as our primary engine — because it's the most consistent and accessible.
  • You will learn the principles, not just the buttons.
