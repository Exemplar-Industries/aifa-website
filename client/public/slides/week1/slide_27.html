The Manifesto

Principle 4: Build in Public

"Your portfolio is your resume. Nobody cares about your degree; they care about what you published yesterday."

The AI community moves too fast to work in isolation. If you hide your work until it's perfect, you will be left behind.

  • Share the Process People connect with the journey. Post your failures, your prompts, and your work-in-progress.
  • Embrace Feedback The community will spot flaws you missed and suggest techniques you haven't tried.
  • Attract Opportunity When you build in public, clients and collaborators find you. Visibility creates luck.
