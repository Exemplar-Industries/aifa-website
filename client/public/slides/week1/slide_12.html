The AI Tool Explosion

There Are Hundreds of AI Tools.
Most of Them Don't Matter for Filmmaking.

Since 2022, the number of AI creative tools has exploded. There are now dozens of AI image generators, video generators, lip sync tools, upscalers, audio generators, and aggregator platforms. New tools launch every week.

The key is knowing which tools actually matter for professional AI filmmaking — and which ones are just noise.

  • 100+ AI video/image tools exist as of 2025
  • Most tools are redundant, unreliable, or not optimized for filmmaking workflows
  • The tools that matter fall into 3 categories: AI Image, AI Video, and Aggregators
  • Chasing every new tool is the fastest way to never finish a project
