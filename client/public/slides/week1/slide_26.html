The Manifesto

Principle 3: Tell Stories

03
"Nobody cares about your hyper-realistic 4K AI video if the story is boring."
The Tech Trap
  • Obsessing over prompt exactness and pixel-perfect details
  • Chasing 60fps smooth motion just because the tool can do it
  • Showing off the software's capabilities instead of a narrative
The Filmmaker's Focus
  • Establishing clear character motivation and stakes
  • Creating genuine conflict that drives the scene forward
  • Eliciting an emotional response from the audience
