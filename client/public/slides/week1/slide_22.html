Mindset & Identity

This Lesson Is Different

We've talked about the industry. We've talked about the tools.
Now, we need to talk about you.

The AI space is flooded with people who know how to type prompts but don't know how to tell a story. Tools don't make films. Filmmakers make films. To succeed, you must adopt the identity of a creator.

The "Prompt Engineer"
Obsesses over the latest tool updates, generates random cool images, never finishes a cohesive project.
The AI Filmmaker
Uses tools to serve a narrative, follows a strict workflow, and actually publishes completed films.
