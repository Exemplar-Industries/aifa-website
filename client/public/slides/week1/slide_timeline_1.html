The History

The Evolution of AI Media: 2022–2024

2022

The Beginning

  • I discover AI tools for a client project
  • Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, ComfyUI — all very complex
  • Runway exists but barely usable
  • Heavy morphing, blurry outputs, bad eyes
  • Very low motion — not production-ready
2023

Early Progress

  • Gradual quality improvements across tools
  • Motion becoming slightly more controlled
  • Still not great — not what we have today
  • Midjourney emerges as the top image generator
  • Best creativity, colors, and artistic range
2024

The Turning Point

  • Runway Gen 2 & Gen 3 launch — big leap
  • Motion noticeably more usable for real projects
  • Midjourney solidifies as the top AI image tool
  • Agencies and studios start paying attention
  • The race to production quality begins
