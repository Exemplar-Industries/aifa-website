The 3 Types of Tools

Every Tool in Your Stack Falls
Into One of Three Categories

Category 1
AI Image
Generation
Creates still images from text prompts. This is the foundation of your visual assets — characters, environments, and props.
Examples
Nano Banana Pro
Midjourney
GPT Image
Category 2
AI Video
Generation
Animates still images or generates video directly from text prompts. This brings your static scenes to life.
Examples
Google Veo 3
Kling AI
Runway ML
Category 3
Aggregators &
Editing Tools
Platforms that combine multiple AI tools or help you manage, edit, polish, and export your final work.
Examples
HiggsField
OpenArt
CapCut
Adobe After Effects
