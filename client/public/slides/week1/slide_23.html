The Shift

Stop Watching. Start Making.

The biggest trap in AI is scrolling through feeds, watching what other people make, and thinking you are learning. You are not learning. You are consuming. To succeed, you must cross the chasm from passive consumer to active creator.

The Consumer
The Creator
Watches endless tutorials without taking action.
Opens the software and breaks things to learn.
Complains about tool limitations and bugs.
Finds creative workarounds for current limitations.
Waits for the "perfect" update to start creating.
Ships finished work with the tools they have today.
