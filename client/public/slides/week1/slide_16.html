What Google Flow Does

The Engine of Our Workflow

Google Flow combines two state-of-the-art models into a single, seamless interface. This allows us to move from concept to final footage without ever leaving the platform.

NB Pro (Image Gen)
The foundation of your visual assets. Generates high-fidelity, photorealistic base images from text prompts.
  • Establish character design and consistency
  • Build cinematic storyboards and shot lists
  • Control lighting, composition, and art direction
Veo 3 (Video Gen)
The animation engine. Takes your base images and brings them to life with broadcast-quality motion.
  • Generate fluid, realistic camera movements
  • Animate complex character actions and physics
  • Output synchronized audio natively with the video
