The 3-5 Second Rule

The golden rule of modern pacing and retention editing.
Never let a single shot linger longer than 5 seconds.

  • Viewer attention spans are shorter than ever.
  • Change the camera angle, cut to B-roll, or introduce motion every 3-5 seconds.
  • This creates a "pattern interrupt" that resets the viewer's attention span.
  • This is why generating multiple angles of the same scene in Production is critical.
